# BUG-002 — LSU-18 clicks the tail and gets the fuselage: its pick points are read while the parts are still moving

- **Date found:** 2026-10-31 · claude-opus-5-5 (reported by the FEATURE-009 session; reproduced 3 of 3 runs, nobody using the mouse)
- **Found by:** claude-opus-5-5
- **Severity:** minor (a verification row fails every run; no student-facing fault found)
- **Status:** verified
- **Module(s):** Visual Lesson Engine (Lesson Surface scene page `Page/scene.js`; `tools/LessonSurfaceSmoke` row LSU-18)

## Replication
1. `LessonSurfaceSmoke --assert-pick-schematic` (moves the real cursor; nobody using the mouse).
2. The F-35B schematic lesson (`tools/LessonSmoke/fixtures/f35b-schematic.json`) plays; the page offers points for
   `jet.fuselage`, `jet.tail`, `jet.lift-fan`.
3. The real click on `jet.tail`'s point comes back as `PartPick(jet, f35b, fuselage)`.

## Expected vs actual
- **Expected:** every offered point picks its own part (as LSU-15 does on the engine, 9/9).
- **Actual:** `jet.tail -> f35b/fuselage`; the other three checks pass.

## Hypotheses
1. 2026-10-31 · claude-opus-5-5 — **The snapshot is taken mid-tween (leading hypothesis, from reading the code).**
   - The lesson's last beat, b6, explodes the jet back from 0.5 to 0. `explode()` in `scene.js` runs a 400 ms tween.
   - The beat's `done` reply waits for one rendered frame, not for the tween. `PauseSpeaker(60)` then ends the
     beat after about 60 ms.
   - So the row's first `DescribeSceneAsync`, right after `PlayAsync`, calls `pickPoints()` while the parts are
     still about halfway back.
   - The numbers fit:
     - The schematic's centre is x = -0.505 (mean of the 10 primitive positions, `Schematics.ToModel`).
     - Exploded, the tail moves about 0.6 toward -x and the fuselage about 0.6 toward +x (`ExplodeDistance` 0.6).
     - Mid-tween, the horizontal tail plane's centre (x = -1.4, z = 0, mesh `#0`, the first mesh `pickPoints`
       tries) is past the fuselage's back end. The steady check passes and the point is offered.
     - Once at rest, that centre (y 0.05) sits under the fuselage capsule (x from -1.6 to 1.6, top at y 0.25).
       From the top view a click there hits the fuselage.
   - It also explains why LSU-15 passes: its engine lesson is one `model` beat with no tween.
   - Ruled out by reading: DPI and rounding (`Click`/`Norm`, `ToScreen`). These are shared with LSU-15, and the
     steady check allows 2 px. The solid-vs-any fallback in `pickPart` is the same function for the check and the
     click. Glow and picking on/off do not change what `scene.pick` hits.
   - To confirm without the mouse: describe right after `PlayAsync`, then again after the render loop stops, and
     compare `jet.tail`'s point.

2. 2026-10-31 · claude-opus-5-5 — **Confirmed** with a temporary, uncommitted `--diag-still` mode (no mouse).
   - It played the lesson, then described the scene every 100 ms:
     - t+0: frames 64, `jet.tail@1001,423`
     - t+100: frames 83, `jet.tail@1010,456`
     - t+300: frames 119
     - from t+400: frames 120 and points unchanged, `jet.tail@1013,458`
   - At rest the tail still has a point, but a different one (another of its meshes), 35 px lower.
   - The fuselage and lift-fan points moved a few pixels too.

## Root cause
LSU-18 was wrong, not the product.
- The row read `PickPoints` as soon as `LessonPlayer.PlayAsync` returned. The lesson's last beat was still
  tweening the jet back together.
- The scene page renders on demand. A beat's `done` waits for one frame, not for the beat's tweens, so the
  lesson "ends" up to 400 ms before the parts stop.
- The points described a mid-tween scene. The tail's mid-tween point lands on the fuselage once the parts are at rest.
- A student is not affected. The real click handler picks what is drawn at the moment of the click.

## Fix
- `tools/LessonSurfaceSmoke/LessonSurfaceSmokeHost.Pick.cs` — new `UntilStillAsync`.
  - It polls the scene's frame count every 100 ms until it holds still, which means the render loop has stopped:
    no tween, no running animation. It gives up after about 3 s.
  - LSU-15 now waits for it too, and its first check requires it (the engine lesson has no tween, so no change there).
- `tools/LessonSurfaceSmoke/LessonSurfaceSmokeHost.PickSchematic.cs` — LSU-18 waits for the scene to be still
  before it reads the points. Its first check now also requires "at rest".
- No check was loosened. The scene page and the product are unchanged.
- Rejected:
  - Making a beat's `done` wait for its tweens: it changes every lesson's pacing.
  - Having `pickPoints()` offer nothing while the scene moves: it changes the product page for a test-only
    concern, and "moving" would read the same as "nothing visible".
