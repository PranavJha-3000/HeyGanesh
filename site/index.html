विघ्नहर्ता means the remover of obstacles. Every learner hits a wall: the term nobody explained, the diagram that never clicked, the menu you cannot find. Hey Ganesh stands next to you and clears it, out loud, on your own screen.

Three steps, no window to open

  1. Hold Ctrl+Alt

    Press and hold both keys anywhere in Windows. Hey Ganesh sits in the notification area until you do.

  2. Ask out loud

    Say what you want to know, like “where is the Save button?” or “explain this graph”, then pause.

  3. Watch the answer

    A box lands on the thing you asked about, or a drawing builds on your screen while a voice talks you through it.

It looks at your screen, so you don't have to describe it

Finds it for you

Hey Ganesh reads what is on the screen and outlines the button, menu or field you asked about. It never clicks or types for you. You stay in control.

Explains out loud

Questions about an idea get a short spoken answer, written with the thing on your screen in mind.

Draws the idea

When a picture helps, such as a lever, a circuit or a shape in 3D, it sketches the diagram over your desktop, timed to the voice. Past lessons replay from the tray icon without asking the AI again.

Listens and speaks with Gnani AI

Add a Gnani key and Hey Ganesh uses two of Gnani's speech models, so you don't have to run anything else yourself. Both are built for Indian languages, and Hey Ganesh is set up for English and Hindi.

Install it in a minute

  1. Download the zip

    Download for Windows

  2. Extract it

    Right-click the zip and choose Extract All.

  3. Run the installer

    Right-click install.ps1 and choose Run with PowerShell. It installs for your account only and needs no admin rights.

  4. Paste your keys

    Open Hey Ganesh from the Start menu. A window asks for your keys the first time. Then hold Ctrl+Alt and ask.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 and a microphone
  • A DeepSeek key and a TypeSafe key (required)
  • A Gnani key for the Prisma and Timbre voice (optional)

Keys are kept in Windows Credential Manager. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you: choose More info, then Run anyway.

If Windows blocks the script, open the folder in Terminal and run:

powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File install.ps1

Questions

What does it see?

The screen the cursor is on, only when you ask. The names and text of what is on that screen, and in some cases a picture of it, go to the AI services whose keys you added. Hey Ganesh does not save screenshots or screen text. It keeps your question, the answer and any drawing in a notes file on your PC.

Can it click or type for me?

No. It only outlines and draws on top of your screen. Your mouse and keyboard stay yours.

Is it safe to run?

The code is public, so you can read it. The installer is not code-signed yet, which is why SmartScreen asks first. It installs to your own account and the uninstaller removes it.

Does it work on Mac, Linux or phones?

Not yet. Hey Ganesh is built for Windows 11 and also runs on Windows 10.

Which languages can I ask in?

English and Hindi, including a mix of the two. Gnani's models cover ten Indian languages, so more can be added.
